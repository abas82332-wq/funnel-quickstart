What the price difference means

At current published monthly pricing, Systeme.io's Startup plan is substantially lower-cost than ClickFunnels' Launch plan. That does not mean one is universally better: the useful choice depends on the specific funnel, audience size, email volume, collaboration and automation requirements.

Who should examine each option?

Systeme.io is worth examining when you want funnels, email, automation and related business tools in one product and want to start on a free plan. ClickFunnels is worth examining when its higher-priced funnel platform and current Launch/Scale/Optimize feature sets match your workflow.
