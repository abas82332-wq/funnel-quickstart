First Funnel Checklist

Print this page or save it as PDF.

  1. I can describe the target audience in one sentence.
  2. I can state the problem the funnel solves.
  3. I have one clear offer.
  4. The first screen explains the outcome and the next step.
  5. The CTA appears without forcing the visitor to hunt for it.
  6. I have a genuine proof point, demo or explanation.
  7. There is a relevant follow-up sequence.
  8. I can measure visits, CTA clicks and sign-ups.
  9. I have tested the complete path on mobile.
  10. I know what single metric I will improve first.
