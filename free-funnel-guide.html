How to Build a Sales Funnel for Free

A simple workflow for a creator who has one offer and wants a clean path from visitor to customer.

1. Choose one problem

Write the funnel around one problem a specific audience already understands. “Get more leads” is broad; “Turn Instagram visitors into booked discovery calls” is easier to communicate.

2. Make one offer

Choose one next step: free trial, consultation, download, webinar registration or purchase. Avoid sending the visitor to five competing choices.

3. Build the landing page

Your first screen should make three things obvious: who it is for, what outcome it promises, and what the visitor should do next. Add proof only when you have genuine proof.

4. Add follow-up

Most visitors do not act immediately. A useful follow-up sequence answers common objections, demonstrates the offer, and gives a clear next step. Keep messages relevant and honest.

5. Measure the path

Track visits, clicks, sign-ups and paid conversions. Fix the weakest step instead of redesigning everything at once.
