What makes a useful first funnel?

The goal is not to build the biggest funnel. It is to make one clear path from a specific problem to a specific action, then measure what happens.

One audience

Choose one type of person with one recognizable problem rather than writing for everyone.

One promise

Explain the practical outcome a visitor should expect, without vague hype or unsupported claims.

One CTA

Remove competing actions. Ask for the next step you actually want the visitor to take.
